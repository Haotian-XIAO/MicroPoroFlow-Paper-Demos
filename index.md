# Welcome to [Xiao & Genet (2026). Effective permeability of double-scale porous media under finite deformation: a three-phase micro-poro-mechanics framework. In preparation.]'s demos!

Demos can be browsed statically but also interactively—to start a session and run the code just click on the rocket icon at the top of a tutorial page and then click on "Binder".
You can also launch a JupyterLab session with all demos: [![Binder](https://mybinder.org/badge_logo.svg)](https://mybinder.org/v2/gh/Haotian-XIAO/MicroPoroFlow-Paper-Demos/main?urlpath=lab/tree/./demos).

Contents:
- Figure 3: linear permeability homogenization of undeformed unit cells, and comparison with the Hashin–Shtrikman bound (Sec. 3.1);
- Figure 4: linearity of the fluid flux with respect to the pressure gradient;
- Figures 5–7: permeability under uniaxial stretching and simple shear;
- Figure 8: gas-pressure loading after uniaxial stretching;
- Figure 9: gas-pressure effects under different deformation modes;
- Figure 10: comparison of the loading modes, and state-function test of the reduced permeability models;
- Figure 11: collapse of the permeability onto the current gas fraction (three notebooks: reference curve κ0 of the undeformed unit cells, refined-mesh loading cases, and the figure itself).

The book is built without executing the notebooks. Some notebooks are computationally intensive (up to about two hours); see the [README](https://github.com/Haotian-XIAO/MicroPoroFlow-Paper-Demos#readme) for the running times, the order in which the Figure 11 notebooks must be run, and how to run the demos locally.
